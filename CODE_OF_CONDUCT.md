# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

Maintainers and contributors pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Examples of positive behavior:
- Showing empathy and kindness toward others.
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Sexualized language or imagery; unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, personal/political attacks.
- Public/private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report violations to `nuttaruj@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of reporters.

Maintainers who fail to follow or enforce this Code of Conduct may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other project leadership.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — issues, PRs, discussions, social media when representing the project, and any public event.
